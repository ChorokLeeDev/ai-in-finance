\begin{abstract}
Discovering causal relationships in financial time series is challenging because causal structures change across market regimes. Existing neural causal discovery methods assume stationary causal graphs, while regime-switching models typically focus on prediction without learning causal structure. We propose \textbf{\rancd{}} (Regime-Aware Neural Causal Discovery), the first neural architecture that jointly learns latent regime dynamics and regime-conditional causal graphs. Our model combines: (1) per-factor temporal encoders that capture individual time series dynamics, (2) a Transformer-based regime encoder that discovers latent market states, (3) a regime-conditioned graph structure learner, and (4) NOTEARS-style acyclicity constraints for valid DAG learning. On synthetic data with known ground truth, \rancd{} achieves superior causal discovery accuracy compared to baselines. On Fama-French 6-factor data, our model reveals interpretable regime-dependent causal structures that align with economic theory, with distinct patterns during crisis and normal periods. Our work enables practitioners to understand how factor relationships evolve across market conditions, supporting more robust risk management and portfolio construction.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Understanding causal relationships between financial factors is crucial for portfolio management, risk monitoring, and market analysis. Traditional approaches to causal discovery, such as Granger causality testing \cite{granger1969investigating} and vector autoregression (VAR), assume that causal relationships remain constant over time. However, financial markets exhibit distinct regimes---bull markets, bear markets, and crisis periods---where the causal structure between factors can fundamentally change.

Consider the relationship between size (SMB) and value (HML) factors. During normal market conditions, these factors may operate relatively independently. During crisis periods, however, flight-to-quality behavior can create strong causal linkages as investors simultaneously exit small-cap and value positions. Such regime-dependent causality cannot be captured by methods assuming stationary causal graphs.

Recent advances in neural causal discovery \cite{tank2021neural} enable nonlinear Granger causality testing using deep learning. The NOTEARS framework \cite{zheng2018dags} provides continuous optimization for DAG learning. Neural Relational Inference \cite{kipf2018neural} learns interaction graphs from trajectories. However, none of these methods model how causal structure varies across latent regimes. Separately, deep switching state space models \cite{xu2021deep} excel at regime discovery for forecasting but do not extract causal graphs.

\textbf{Our contribution.} We propose \rancd{}, a novel architecture that bridges this gap by jointly learning:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Latent market regimes through temporal encoding
    \item Regime-conditional causal graphs via neural structure learning
    \item Time-varying causal strengths with interpretable attention
\end{enumerate}

Our key insight is that factor embeddings, regime states, and causal edges can be learned end-to-end through a unified objective combining prediction loss, DAG constraints, and regime smoothness.

\section{Related Work}

\subsection{Neural Causal Discovery}
Tank et al. \cite{tank2021neural} introduced component-wise MLPs and LSTMs with group-lasso penalties for neural Granger causality. Their method achieves automatic lag selection but assumes stationary causal structure. The NOTEARS algorithm \cite{zheng2018dags} reformulates DAG learning as continuous optimization via the acyclicity constraint $h(W) = \text{tr}(e^{W \circ W}) - d = 0$. We incorporate this constraint into our neural architecture.

\subsection{Graph Neural Networks for Dynamics}
Kipf et al.'s Neural Relational Inference \cite{kipf2018neural} learns interaction graphs from observed trajectories using a VAE framework. However, NRI assumes a static interaction graph throughout the sequence.

\subsection{Regime-Switching Models}
DS$^3$M \cite{xu2021deep} combines RNNs with switching state space models for time series forecasting. While effective for regime discovery, it does not extract causal graphs. Markov-switching VAR models capture regime-dependent dynamics but are limited to linear relationships.

\subsection{Causal Discovery in Finance}
PCMCI \cite{runge2019detecting} provides constraint-based causal discovery for time series but uses discrete tests rather than continuous optimization. Recent work applies these methods to climate and neuroscience rather than financial applications.

\textbf{Gap:} No existing method combines neural causal discovery with explicit regime modeling. \rancd{} is the first to learn regime-conditional causal graphs.

\section{Methodology}

\subsection{Problem Formulation}
Given multivariate financial time series $\mathbf{X} = (\mathbf{x}_1, \ldots, \mathbf{x}_T)$ where $\mathbf{x}_t \in \mathbb{R}^n$ represents $n$ factors at time $t$, we aim to learn:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Latent regime sequence $\mathbf{z} = (z_1, \ldots, z_T)$ where $z_t \in \{1, \ldots, K\}$
    \item Regime-conditional causal graphs $\{G^{(k)}\}_{k=1}^K$ where $G^{(k)}$ is a DAG over the $n$ factors
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Architecture Overview}

\rancd{} consists of four components:

\paragraph{Factor Encoder} Each factor $i$ is encoded independently using an LSTM:
\begin{equation}
    \mathbf{h}_i^{(t)} = \text{LSTM}_i(\mathbf{x}_{1:t,i})
\end{equation}
Temporal attention pools over time to produce factor embeddings $\mathbf{e}_i$.

\paragraph{Regime Encoder} A Transformer encoder processes the full multivariate series:
\begin{equation}
    \mathbf{r}_t = \text{softmax}(\text{MLP}(\text{Transformer}(\mathbf{X})_t))
\end{equation}
producing regime probabilities $\mathbf{r}_t \in \Delta^{K-1}$.

\paragraph{Graph Structure Learner} Edge probabilities are computed via regime-conditioned MLPs:
\begin{equation}
    A_{ij} = \sigma\left(\text{MLP}([\mathbf{e}_i; \mathbf{e}_j; \bar{\mathbf{r}}])\right)
\end{equation}
where $\bar{\mathbf{r}}$ is the time-averaged regime distribution.

\paragraph{Causal Predictor} Predictions are made using the learned graph as a mask:
\begin{equation}
    \hat{\mathbf{x}}_{t+1,j} = f_j\left(\sum_{i: A_{ij} > \tau} A_{ij} \cdot \mathbf{x}_{t-L:t,i}\right)
\end{equation}

\subsection{Training Objective}

We optimize a combined loss:
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{L} = \lambda_1 \mathcal{L}_{\text{pred}} + \lambda_2 \mathcal{L}_{\text{DAG}} + \lambda_3 \mathcal{L}_{\text{sparse}} + \lambda_4 \mathcal{L}_{\text{regime}}
\end{equation}

where:
\begin{itemize}
    \item $\mathcal{L}_{\text{pred}} = \|\hat{\mathbf{X}} - \mathbf{X}\|_2^2$ (prediction MSE)
    \item $\mathcal{L}_{\text{DAG}} = \text{tr}(e^{A \circ A}) - n$ (NOTEARS constraint)
    \item $\mathcal{L}_{\text{sparse}} = \|A\|_1$ (sparsity)
    \item $\mathcal{L}_{\text{regime}} = \sum_t \|\mathbf{r}_{t+1} - \mathbf{r}_t\|_2^2$ (smoothness)
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Fama-French Factors}
We apply \rancd{} to daily Fama-French 6-factor returns (1990-2024):
\begin{itemize}
    \item Mkt-RF (market excess return)
    \item SMB (small minus big)
    \item HML (high minus low)
    \item RMW (robust minus weak)
    \item CMA (conservative minus aggressive)
    \item Mom (momentum)
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Key Finding:} The learned causal graph changes substantially between regimes. During crisis periods (2008, 2020), we observe:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Increased centrality of market factor (Mkt-RF)
    \item Stronger SMB $\to$ HML linkage (flight from small value)
    \item Emergence of Mom as causal driver
\end{itemize}

\section{Discussion}

\paragraph{Limitations}
\begin{itemize}
    \item RANCD requires specification of the number of regimes $K$
    \item Training is more expensive than linear methods
    \item Causal interpretation requires domain expertise
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Future Work}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Automatic regime number selection via BIC
    \item Extension to intraday and high-frequency data
    \item Integration with portfolio optimization
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}

We presented \rancd{}, the first neural architecture for regime-aware causal discovery in financial networks. By jointly learning latent regimes and regime-conditional causal graphs, our method reveals how factor relationships evolve across market conditions. This capability supports more nuanced risk management and factor-based investing strategies. Our work opens new directions at the intersection of causal discovery, deep learning, and financial time series analysis.
